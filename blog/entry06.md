To prepare for this presentation I made a [plan](https://github.com/darrenl5941/sep11-freedom-project/blob/main/prep/presentation.md) so I could sep up my presenation and the order in which I wanted to say or show things. Once I made my plan, I made the [slideshow](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1HPa_sv7b60Mlwql0J59tL15lKQmgksQCFUiRvqNvJAI/edit?slide=id.p#slide=id.p) for my presentation.

I also could have practiced my presentation more so I would have better pacing throughout my presentation and not have to rush slightly towards the end of my presentation.
