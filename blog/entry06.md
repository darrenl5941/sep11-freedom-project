### Engineering Design Process (EDP)

I am currently on _part 8_ of the **Engineering Design Process**, communicating the results. I have already fully completed my [project](https://darrenl5941.github.io/sep11-freedom-project/) and have been showing how I made it and what it does. I have shown my project in a **presentation in class** and in the **SEP Expo Elevator Pitch** (Software Engineering Program) in my school. 

### Presenting my Project

#### In-Class Presentation

The first time I showed of my project was during the **in-class presentation** where I explained how I made my project, the challenges I had with it, and what my project does in a 10-minute presenation. To prepare for this presentation I made a [plan](https://github.com/darrenl5941/sep11-freedom-project/blob/main/prep/presentation.md) so I could sep up my presenation and the order in which I wanted to say or show things. Once I made my plan, I made the [slideshow](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1HPa_sv7b60Mlwql0J59tL15lKQmgksQCFUiRvqNvJAI/edit?slide=id.p#slide=id.p) for my presentation.

In this presentation there were multiple points where I could have improved. I should have changed the order in which I presented things, I presented my code before actually showing off my project when I should have presented them the other way around so the people watching my presentation could better understand what the code I was showing did. I also could have practiced my presentation more so I would have better pacing throughout my presentation and not have to rush slightly towards the end of my presentation.

#### Expo Elevator Pitch

During the **Expo Elevator Pitch** I had 1 minute to explain and show off my project to multiple judges. There were multiple points at which I could have improved at during my short presentation, such as wording my presentation in a way that was easier to understand as one of the judges looked confused while I was explaining my project.

### Skills

#### Organization

With my _in-class presentation_ I learned how important it is to _organize your ideas_. In my presentation it was difficult for some of my peers to understand the code that I was showing because I showed it before showing what my project actually is. Organizing anything you are showing to others is extremely important so they can have a good understanding of what they are trying to show to them.

#### Practicing

Another important skill that I learned was the _importance of practicing_. Before my in class presentation I did not practice my presentation much, which caused my pacing throughout my presentation to be off to be able to fit into the 10 minute timeframe my presentation was supposed to have. Practicing is an extremely important skill to have as you need to do it to be able to see what changes you need to make in what you are doing and so when it comes time to actually doing what you need to do it can flow smoothly.

#### Communication

In my elevator pitch I learned how important it is to be able to _communicate clearly_. In my elevator pitch one of the judges was a little confused by my project because fo how I explained and worded it. Being able to communicate clearly is important as it allows others to understand what you are saying and without good communication it can lead to people getting confused.
